Frost Mage Leveling Guide: Levels 1 to 20

WoW Forever

Skills, Talents and Rotation (Frost)

Visit your trainer every even level and buy every new rank. Everything below is all you need to kill mobs from 1 to 20.

3. Before every pull

  • Buffs: Frost Armor and Arcane Intellect up
  • Mana: drink conjured water to full
  • Wand: grab one as soon as you can. It saves a ton of mana.

4. Rotation: levels 1 to 9

  1. Pull with Frostbolt (Fireball until level 4)
  2. Keep casting Frostbolt while it walks in slowed
  3. Fire Blast to finish it (from level 6)
  4. Wand or melee if you're low on mana
Frostbolt → Frostbolt → Fire Blast

5. Rotation: level 10+

  1. Pull with Frostbolt from max range
  2. Frostbolt again while it's slowed
  3. When it reaches you, Frost Nova and step back
  4. Frostbolt again, then Fire Blast or wand to finish
Frostbolt → Frostbolt → Nova → Back up → Frostbolt → Fire Blast

Level 20: spam Ice Lance while it's frozen from Nova or Frostbite. Blink out if Nova is on cooldown, Evocation when you're out of mana.

6. If it goes bad

  • Polymorph the add, kill the first mob, then the sheep
  • Frost Nova and run to get distance
  • Blink away if you're stuck (20+)
  • Mana Shield to soak damage while you get away (20+)

7. Quick reminders

  • Pull one mob at a time. Adds are the #1 killer.
  • Never let mobs sit on you. Nova and back up.
  • Conjure food and water, never buy it.
Frostbolt, Frostbolt, Nova, back up, finish it.
