3. Before every pull

  • Aura: Devotion Aura (1–15), Retribution Aura (16+)
  • Buff: Blessing of Might on yourself
  • Seal: Seal of Righteousness (1–19), Seal of Command (20+)

4. Rotation: levels 1 to 19

  1. Keep Seal of Righteousness up. Judging doesn't eat it anymore.
  2. Judgement as you run in (from level 4)
  3. Holy Strike (from level 6)
  4. Auto attack, and use Judgement and Holy Strike every time they're off cooldown
Seal → Judge → Holy Strike → Swing

5. Rotation: level 20 to 35

  1. Keep Seal of Command up instead of Righteousness
  2. Judgement as you run in, then Holy Strike on cooldown
  3. Auto attack. A slow two-hander makes Seal of Command hit way harder.
  4. Exorcism on undead or demons when it's off cooldown
  5. Consecration on packs. Group them up first, it only hits 4 at full damage.
Command → Judge → Holy Strike → Swing

Level 33: Repentance or Hammer of Justice the dangerous mob.

6. If it goes bad

  • Hammer of Justice to stun, then heal with Flash of Light or Holy Light
  • Divine Shield when you're about to die (34+). Divine Protection before that.
  • Lay on Hands as a last resort
  • Blessing of Freedom if you're rooted or slowed (18+)

7. Quick reminders

  • Pull one mob at a time. Adds are the #1 killer.
  • Skip Seal of Fury and Righteous Fury solo. They're for tanking.
  • Concentration Aura (22) stops pushback while healing.
Seal stays up. Judge, Holy Strike, swing, repeat.
