Rogue Leveling Guide: Levels 1 to 35

WoW Forever

Skills, Talents and Rotation (Combat)

Visit your trainer every even level and buy every new rank. Everything below is all you need to kill mobs from 1 to 35.

3. Before every pull

  • Stealth up and walk in behind it
  • Energy: wait for full energy before you open
  • Poisons: keep them on both weapons once you have them

4. Rotation: levels 1 to 9

  1. Sinister Strike until you have 3–5 combo points
  2. Eviscerate to finish
  3. Repeat. Gouge (6+) buys you a free breather.
Sinister Strike ×3–5 → Eviscerate

5. Rotation: level 10 to 35

  1. Open from Stealth with Garrote (14+) or Cheap Shot (26+)
  2. Sinister Strike to 1–2 combo points, then Slice and Dice
  3. Sinister Strike back up to 5 combo points, then Eviscerate
  4. Riposte whenever it lights up after a parry
  5. Two mobs at 30+: Blade Flurry and hit both
Open → SS → Slice and Dice → SS ×3 → Eviscerate

Level 30: Kidney Shot at 5 points stops a mob that's about to flee or cast.

6. If it goes bad

  • Gouge or Kidney Shot (30+) to stop the damage
  • Evasion when two mobs are on you
  • Sprint, then Vanish (22+) to reset the fight
  • Blind the add (34+), Kick casters (12+)

7. Quick reminders

  • Pull one mob at a time. Adds are the #1 killer.
  • Skin everything you kill. That's your gold and your leather.
  • Sap (10+) a mob from stealth to deal with packs one at a time.

Gnome rogue notes

  • Where you start: Coldridge Valley in Dun Morogh. Your rogue trainer after that is Hogral Bakkan, at the back of the Thunderbrew Distillery in Kharanos. From about 20 on, train in Ironforge (Forlorn Cavern).
  • Escape Artist: breaks roots and slows (Frost Nova, Hamstring, nets). Use it to run away, or to catch someone who slowed you.
  • Engineering Specialization: +15 Engineering. Engineering is the best rogue profession for PvP (bombs, nets, target dummies, and guns for your ranged slot). Pair it with Mining.
  • Skip the rest: Expansive Mind (+5% Intellect) does nothing for a rogue, and the +10 Arcane Resistance only helps a little against mages.
  • Leveling instead of PvP? Skinning + Leatherworking makes most of the gear in section 8, then you can switch to Engineering at 60.
