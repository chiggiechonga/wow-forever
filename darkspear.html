6How a match works

Basically Arathi Basin (hold bases) mixed with Eye of the Storm (run a flag). First team to 2,000 points wins.

ThingWhat to know
Teams15 vs 15. You start at a stone fort at the north end of the northern island.
Abandoned TowerBase on the east side of the northern island. Defenders stand on top, so it's very hard to take with melee. Bring ranged or crowd control.
Shipwreck CoveCoastal base by the wrecked ships.
RuinsTroll ruins with the capture flag out in the open.
CampTroll camp in the red hills of the southern island (Horde side).
CapturingClick the base's flag and stay close while it turns. Every base you hold adds points each tick, so more bases = faster points.
Center flagSpawns in the middle. Carry it to any base your team holds for a burst of points. It's worth more the more bases you hold. The carrier runs at only 85% speed, so they need escorts.
The water⚠️ Falling in the sea = shark attack = dead. Only bridges and walkways connect the islands. Stay away from edges near knockbacks.
Base names come from beta and could have changed. Per-tick and flag point values haven't been published.

7Your job as a priest

Healers win this map. Here's what to do, in order of what matters most.

#Do thisWhy
1Stay with the main group and move onto one base togetherSmall split groups get picked off. One big push takes a base fast.
2Escort the flag carrier: Shield, Renew, dispel slowsThey're slowed to 85%. Escorts should heal and remove debuffs before doing damage.
3If you defend, stay on the baseA base with nobody on it can be flipped by one stealthed rogue. About 2 people per base is a good default. Don't chase people far off the point.
4Call out attacks earlySay how many are coming and which base, e.g. "3 inc Tower".
5Watch your footing on bridgesBridges are chokepoints. Psychic Scream works great there, but stay out of knockback range near the edges.
6Play the objective, not killsPoints come from bases + flag. When you're ahead, pulling 2–3 enemies off to a far corner is worth it.

8Turn in your marks (repeat)

This is the loop: play, get marks, hand in 3 at a time. You can only hold 30, so don't sit on them.

LoopWhat happens
Win3 marks → 1 Clash turn-in (+100–150 rep) = about 100–150 rep. Your first win each day also gets the win quest (+200 rep), so that win is worth about 300–350 rep.
Loss1 mark. Three losses = 1 Clash turn-in (+100–150 rep)
Cap30 marks max. That's 10 turn-ins waiting, so empty your bag at the camp or in a city.
The win quest is once a day (resets 7 AM Pacific), so never skip your first win of the day. Blizzard hasn't said how much rep a match gives by itself, so these numbers only count the quests.
