⚔️ Rotations

What to press in each situation, using your Naowh keys. Spells with a level next to them unlock later. Until then, skip that step.

🎯 One normal mob your bread and butter

  1. Shift+V Shadowform 42. Turn it on once and leave it on
  2. 2 SW: Pain. Pull with it from max range
  3. 3 Mind Blast
  4. 1 Mind Flay 22 until it's dead. Before 22, 4 wand instead
  5. Q Shield if it reaches you and you're under ~60% HP

💡 Low mana? Finish mobs with the wand 4. Spirit Tap gives big mana regen after a kill, so let it tick before drinking.

👥 Two or three mobs accidental pulls

  1. Q Shield yourself first
  2. 2 SW: Pain on every mob (tab target)
  3. Shift+Q Vampiric Embrace 27 on the main target, so your damage heals you
  4. 1 Mind Flay the main one, 3 Mind Blast on cooldown
  5. 3+ mobs and dropping fast: G Psychic Scream. Kill one while they run
  6. Still dying: Caps Desperate Prayer, then R Lesser Heal / Shift+R Flash Heal

💡 Feared mobs can run into more mobs. Scream only when you have to.

💪 Elite or tough mob quest bosses, rares

  1. Buff up: Fortitude + Inner Fire, full mana
  2. 5 Holy Fire as the opener from max range. Skip it in Shadowform
  3. 2 SW: Pain, 6 Devouring Plague, Shift+Q Vampiric Embrace 27
  4. Q Shield before it reaches you, and re-Shield every time Weakened Soul drops off
  5. 3 Mind Blast on cooldown, 1 Mind Flay in between
  6. Emergency: Caps Desperate Prayer · Shift+G Stoneform (vs poison/bleed)

💀 Undead Duskwood, Plaguelands

  1. 2 undead? Shift+X Shackle Undead the 2nd one first
  2. Kill the other with the normal rotation
  3. Re-shackle if it breaks. Never DoT the shackled one, since damage breaks it

🧙 Caster mobs they run away from melee

  1. Shift+1 Silence 35 as they start a big cast
  2. 2 SW: Pain + 3 Mind Blast. Casters have low HP
  3. T Dispel buffs off them if they shield themselves

🗡️ Rogue opens on you stunned out of nowhere

  1. When the stun ends: Q Shield right away
  2. Shift+G Stoneform. It removes poisons + bleeds and gives armor
  3. G Psychic Scream, then 2 SW: Pain + 6 Plague while they run
  4. 3 Mind Blast, 1 Mind Flay to slow them when they come back
  5. Low? Caps Desperate Prayer (instant)

🪓 Warrior / melee keep them off you

  1. 2 SW: Pain + 6 Plague as they run at you
  2. Q Shield before they reach you
  3. G Psychic Scream when they're on you. DoTs tick while they run
  4. 1 Mind Flay slows them. Walk backwards + keep flaying
  5. Shift+6 Fear Ward on yourself before the fight vs warriors (they fear too)

🔥 Casters mage, warlock, priest

  1. Shift+6 Fear Ward on yourself vs warlocks + priests, BEFORE the fight
  2. Q Shield to soak their opener
  3. Shift+1 Silence 35 on their big cast (Fireball, Shadow Bolt, Heal)
  4. T Dispel their buffs (Ice Barrier, Mana Shield, Power Word: Shield)
  5. Shift+2 Mana Burn when they're low on mana and you're safe to cast. It drains them dry

🏹 Hunter annoying at range

  1. Q Shield, then run TO them. Hunters can't shoot you in close range
  2. 2 SW: Pain on the hunter, not the pet
  3. Shift+2 Mana Burn. Hunters need mana for their shots
  4. G Psychic Scream hits the pet + hunter

🏃 Ganked by someone way higher don't fight, survive

  1. Q Shield + E Renew
  2. G Psychic Scream, then run toward guards or a town
  3. Caps Desperate Prayer when low
  4. If you die, wait before rezzing. Corpse campers get bored

💡 Fade F does nothing vs players. It only works on mobs.

💚 Healing a friend in a fight

  1. Q Shield them first, it's instant
  2. E Renew, then Shift+R Flash Heal if they're dropping fast
  3. T Dispel slows/fears/sheep off them
  4. G Psychic Scream if the enemies come for you

💚 Healing (your main job)

  1. Before the pull: Fortitude on everyone + Inner Fire on you (your buff addon reminds you), drink to full
  2. Tank pulls: Q / Mouse 4 Shield the tank, then wait 2-3 sec so you don't steal aggro
  3. Steady damage: E / Mouse 3 Renew on the tank and keep it rolling
  4. Big hits: Shift+E Heal (Greater Heal at 40). Slow but mana-friendly
  5. Whole group hurt: Shift+C Prayer of Healing 30
  6. Someone about to die: Shift+R Flash Heal + Q Shield them
  7. Nobody needs heals: 4 wand

🚨 When things go wrong

  1. Mob on you: F Fade, then walk toward the tank
  2. Pack about to wipe the group: G Psychic Scream. Last resort
  3. Tank has a debuff (curse/magic): T Dispel · disease: Shift+5 Cure Disease
  4. Undead add loose: Shift+X Shackle it
  5. Out of mana on a boss: say "OOM" in chat, Caps Desperate Prayer if you're the one dying

🗡️ If you're DPS instead

  1. Wait for 2-3 tank hits
  2. 2 SW: Pain on the kill target only, 1 Mind Flay
  3. Skip Mind Blast early in the pull, since it makes a lot of threat
  4. Shift+Q Vampiric Embrace 27 heals the whole party for free
  5. Pull aggro? F Fade right away
From 40 on: swap to your Holy spec before every dungeon. Greater Heal takes Heal's spot on Shift+E.
Mana tip: stand still for 5 sec after a cast and your mana regen kicks in (the "5 second rule"). Wanding doesn't count as casting, so wand between heals.

🔌 Addon + sync

The ForeverGuide addon reads your level, XP, zone, quest log, turned-in quests, gear, hearth, bag space and AH prices (from Auctioneer, Auctionator or TSM), then gives you a code to paste here.

  1. Open GitHub → new fine-grained token.
  2. Repository access: Only select repositories → wow-forever.
  3. Permissions → Repository → Contents: Read and write. Generate, copy it.
  4. Paste it below. It stays in this browser only, never in the site.
INSTALL THE ADDON one time

⬇ Download ForeverGuide.zip

1. Unzip

Inside is a folder called ForeverGuide.

2. Drop it in

Put that folder in your WoW Forever Interface\AddOns folder. In the Battle.net app, hit the gear next to Play → Show in Explorer, then open the Forever game folder → Interface → AddOns. Make the folders if they're missing.

3. Turn it on

At character select, click AddOns and make sure ForeverGuide is ticked. If it says out of date, tick Load out of date AddOns.

4. Sync

In game, type /fg. A box pops up with your code already selected. Press Ctrl+C, come here, paste it above, hit Sync.

When to sync

Whenever you want a fresh "right now" list: after a batch of turn-ins, at an inn, after you level. Takes 5 seconds.

Heads up: the addon can only remember quests you turn in after you install it. Quests you did before that won't show as synced, so check those off by hand once and you're set.
