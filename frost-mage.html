WoW Forever

Skills, Talents and Rotation (Frost)

3. Before every pull

  • Buffs: Frost Armor (Ice Armor from 30) and Arcane Intellect up
  • Mana: drink conjured water to full. Mana Agate (28) is your emergency refill.
  • Wand: grab one as soon as you can. It saves a ton of mana.

4. Rotation: levels 1 to 19

  1. Pull with Frostbolt (Fireball until level 4)
  2. Frostbolt again while it walks in slowed
  3. At 10+, Frost Nova when it reaches you and step back
  4. Frostbolt again, then Fire Blast or wand to finish
Frostbolt → Frostbolt → Nova → Back up → Finish

5. Rotation: level 20 to 35

  1. Pull with Frostbolt from max range
  2. Frost Nova when it reaches you, then spam Ice Lance while it's frozen
  3. Frostbolt or wand to finish
  4. Packs at 26+: Nova, back up, Cone of Cold, then Arcane Explosion or Blizzard
Frostbolt → Nova → Ice Lance → Ice Lance → Finish

Out of mana: Evocation, then Mana Agate.

6. If it goes bad

  • Polymorph the add, kill the first mob, then the sheep
  • Blink away if Nova is on cooldown (20+)
  • Mana Shield to soak damage while you get away (20+)
  • Ice Block when you're about to die (25+)
  • Counterspell casters that are hitting you hard (24+)

7. Quick reminders

  • Pull one mob at a time unless you're ready to AoE.
  • Never let mobs sit on you. Nova and back up.
  • Conjure food and water, never buy it.
Frostbolt, Nova, Ice Lance while it's frozen, finish it.
